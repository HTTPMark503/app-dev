## 5 favorites movies/series on The List

4. Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse
5. Drawing Closer

---

## Why I Like Them

### 1. Once We're Us
- Heartfelt story
- Characters that feel *real*

### 2. Mushoku Tensei
- Follows **Rudeus Greyrat**, who is reborn in a fantasy world
- Great world-building and magic system
  - Shows a lot of character growth

### 3. Even if This Love Disappears from the World Tonight
- A romance where the girl loses her memories every day
- Very emotional(Not sad at all) 

### 4. Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse
- Follows **Miles Morales** becoming Spider-Man
- The animation style is one of a kind
- Won the Oscar for *Best Animated Feature*

### 5. Drawing Closer
- A touching story about art, love, and limited time
- Beautiful but heartbreaking
